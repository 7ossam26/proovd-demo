FAQ's

How do I know which affiliates will promote my idea? expand_more
Affiliates are selected based on their audience demographics and past performance in similar categories. You will receive a notification once an affiliate accepts your campaign request.
Can I upgrade my plan later? expand_more
Yes, you can upgrade your plan at any time through your account settings. Upgrades take effect immediately.
Do affiliates have to promote every campaign? expand_more
No, affiliates have the freedom to choose campaigns that align with their brand and audience. This ensures better engagement and results for your campaign.
When will I get paid as an affiliate? expand_more
Payments are processed on a monthly basis, typically by the 15th of the following month, provided you meet the minimum payout threshold.
